Completed Work Sample 1

Not for fear did she lack, but because the many dead souls that occupied her flesh so demanded she never once let even a drop of its essence enter their treasured domain. None once did at her great expense, and their recompense was what hideous black currents flowed through her veins as she managed to wake up for another day, eyes barely opened as Efjeln quite literally dragged her out of bed and unto the fields, the break of the morrow barely visible over the horizon.
Echoes in a dark room. An ancient parable spoken through creaking wood and inaudible words, the vessels of change brought on by the desires of a handful few—overseers to the world, hateful to those below, greedy for that which has been taken, thirsty for what is to come, eager for flesh., eager for power, eager for currency not touched by fingers- Eager for blood. My blood. The blood that flows within the city. That which flows beneath. Below. Desperate. Eternal. Distant. Alien. Horrible… “Let us finish the preparations now. Yes, the white dress will do, but…ah, this will add such a delightful flare to the whole…” Now come her hands once again as they pretty up what is to be given and sold, another display, another show, the bait and switch to bait out the waiting patrons who expect so much, and at the same time so little, fully aware that the girl on display has already been sold to someone else.
